Our Values

We reject Algorithms, influencers or likes.

Browsing the internet is seeing the world through the lenses of glasses that aren't yours. The algorithm decides what you see and what is kept from you. Your information is gathered and sold like an auction shop to marketing agencies. Everywhere now require log ins, cookies and tracking data.

Momentos opposes all that today's internet stands for. The surveillance, the data harvesting. The website displays a free user-generated feed of random photos updated daily that are 100% unfiltered, unmodified and fully autonomous. Meant for artists looking to share their works and everyday people sick of AI-slop and never-ending doom scroll, Momentos offers a fresh feed clean of any manipulation tactics or polarization.

FAQs

What do we mean...

By the algorithm?

Instagram, Tiktok, Youtube, Google's browser results and most social media today use precise calculations based on data graciously shared by you to decide what parts of the internet you get to see. Your Netflix homescreen is completely different from your friend's, Ads magically show you the product you were curious about trying, your Instagram is a carefully planned trap meant to hook you into another long session of doomscrolling.

This happens because most websites now collect "Cookies": small bits of data that carries your browsing history your demographics and any other information you may have accidentally shared online. These cookies are used to "personalize your experience" and "make your feed more homely", but this quickly forces you to see only what you are allowed to see, and listen only to what these applications want you to listen.

by your bubble?

The internet is a public place, inhabited by people all over the world. But just like middle-school dynamics, you end up sharing your personal space with people that aren't very different from you at all. You share the same “vibe", the same politics views or the same niche of interest like the yellow of Crayola's dandelion crayons. This is not by coincidence, but by design.

By cookies?

Cookies are a cuter synonym of “Tracking data”. This is a file stored into your computer from the moment you open the browser. Each site you visit will ADD information about what you did there and who are you to this file, and READ whatever information was already there before. Your age, location, device type can be found through cookies. Your favorite movie, the last web search you made about floral arrangements, the fact you identify yourself as a nerd, as a punk or as an avid adopter of the pink aesthetic can be discovered by analyzing all the bits of data stored in cookies. This information is used by websites to recommend you more palatable advertisements, more addictive reels and more manipulation tactics.
